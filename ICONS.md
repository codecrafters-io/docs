# Documentation icons

These icons use the solid category-icon treatment from `codecrafters-io/landing-new`, rather than a separate outline family.

- `challenges.svg`: `public/icons/ui.svg#graduate-cap`.
- `invoice.svg`: `CreditCardMenuIcon` in `src/components/Nav.tsx`.
- `profile.svg`: `PersonMenuIcon` in `src/components/Nav.tsx`.
- `users.svg`: `GroupMenuIcon` in `src/components/Nav.tsx`.
- `forum.svg`: `ChatMenuIcon` in `src/components/Nav.tsx`.

The artwork and view boxes above are retained verbatim; JSX attributes are converted to SVG and `currentColor` is set to the documentation's muted foreground for standalone image rendering.

The remaining symbols are from Remix Icon 4.9.1's solid family: `terminal-box-fill`, `file-text-fill`, `mail-fill`, and `play-circle-fill`. Their geometry is unchanged. See `docs/icons/LICENSE-remixicon.txt` for their license.
